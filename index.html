STAGE 01 · THE PREMISE

Turning a concise premise into a narrative means satisfying many constraints at once: global coherence, character development, pacing, tone consistency, emotional progression.

Frontier models reach that quality only after preference alignment, and aligning at that scale is prohibitive. PlotTwist externalises narrative structure into explicit training signals instead, so 3B active parameters compete with systems many times larger.

01 · WORKFLOW

Structured workflow using SLMs for plot generation

A modular framework of three specialised components: an aspect rating reward model, a DPO-trained mixture-of-experts plot generator, and an independent agentic evaluation module.

STAGE 02 · THE POSSIBILITIES BRANCH
THE FRAMEWORK WHAT IS NEW NEW IN THIS WORKThe reward model, the generator and the evaluator are three separate models that never share weights or training data. Nothing that scores a plot has any stake in producing it.

The story-generation machine

One premise enters. Candidate plots branch out, a reward model rates every branch across five dimensions, preference optimisation turns the ranking into a twist in the generator's policy, and an independent jury decides what comes out.

STAGE 03 · THE TWIST
WHAT IS NEW NEW IN THIS WORKNo reinforcement learning loop, and no reward model at inference. The reward model is used once, offline, to rank candidates into preference pairs. After DPO the generator carries the preference in its weights and runs in a single pass.

What happens when the plot takes a different turn?

A → B → C → D A → B → TWIST ↗ E → F

The twist is not in the story. It is in the policy. Ranking four candidates gives a preference signal, and DPO on 160 pairs redirects the generator, with no reinforcement learning loop and no reward model at inference.

STAGE 04 · THE COMPANY OF SMALL MODELS
THE CAST WHAT IS NEW NEW IN THIS WORKPositive-negative prompting. Every rater scores each aspect twice, once for what works and once for what fails, and the rating is the difference. Asking for one verdict invites the positivity bias LLM judges are known for; asking for both poles cancels it.

Twelve models, one 3B narrator

Large models never write the plot. They rate it before training and judge it after. The model that writes has 3B active parameters per token.

EVIDENCE GROUNDING

Every score cites a span quoted verbatim from the plot. 94% to 99% of roughly 478,000 spans verify. Grounding is measured, never enforced by dropping cells.

SAME-FAMILY BIAS AUDIT

Judges favour their own family by 0.82 points. PlotTwist does not benefit: its corrected inflation is negative at −0.47, and removing same-family cells leaves the ranking unchanged.

DELIBERATION CHANGES LITTLE

Agreement moves from α 0.455 to 0.458 and two verdicts flip. The held-out judge reproduces the panel: 0.89 against GPT-4.1, 0.74 against Agents' Room.

STAGE 06 · ALTERNATE REALITIES
QUALITATIVE WALKTHROUGH WHAT IS NEW NEW IN THIS WORKOnly 160 of 5,000 premises become training data. A pair survives only when a frontier model wins outright, scores above 8, and beats the runner-up by 0.5. Alignment here is a curation problem, not a data-volume one.

One premise, four futures

Every premise in the curation set branches into candidate plots. The reward model ranks the branches; only a few premises survive the thresholds and become training signal.

Highest reward of the four. It becomes the accepted response of the preference pair only if a frontier model produced it, its score exceeds 8.0, and it beats the next-best candidate by at least 0.5.

Lowest reward of the four, and therefore the rejected response the generator is trained away from.

THE UNEXPECTED ENDING

Trained on 160 pairs curated this way, the 3B-active generator wins 90.6% of premises against GPT-4.1 and 73.4% against Agents' Room, from a single model and a single pass.

WHAT IT ACTUALLY WRITES
PREMISE

A dedicated detective investigates a murder that implicates his beloved, forcing him through a web of concealed truths, blackmail, and the lengths one will go to protect a dark secret.

Generated by the PlotTwist MoE
3B active parameters · single pass

Detective Aris Thorne, still scarred by the unsolved murder of his wife Elara, finds his world shattered when Councilman Silas Croft is found in his penthouse with a single, precise stab wound to the heart.

The evidence points damningly toward Lena Voss, his former lover. A rare antique switchblade, identical to one he gifted her years earlier, is found clutched in Croft's hand.

Croft was not merely corrupt. He ran a blackmail network, and the encrypted files reveal he had been threatening Lena with evidence tied to Elara's death. The blackmail was never about Lena. It was about Aris.

Elara had been investigating Croft as a journalist, and was killed in a staged accident when she came too close. At the rain-lashed docks Aris dismantles the network, then chooses love over career and confesses to the murder to protect Lena.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
LLM JUDGES, NOT EXPERTS

Plot quality is assessed by language models rather than expert annotators, and LLM judges agree only moderately with expert readers on creative writing.

ONE COMPARISON UNRESOLVED

The comparison with Claude Sonnet 4 remains statistically unresolved at 160 premises, and correlated juror errors reduce the number of effectively independent opinions.

NARROW SCOPE

English, synopsis-style plots, five craft-oriented dimensions. Reward and preference data are built offline with the aid of larger models, and novelty is left to future work.
